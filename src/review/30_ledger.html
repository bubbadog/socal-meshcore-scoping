Claim ledger

Every checkable statement of fact in the plan, in the plan's order, with a verdict, the evidence, and wording to use instead where the original does not hold. Recommendations and open questions in the plan are not listed; the findings above deal with those.

§01, RT-3Wrong label

"99.5% of observed transport routes were unscoped in the 12 Oct CoreScope snapshot"

[[F]] The analyzer's unscoped total includes every ordinary flood and every direct-routed packet. Transport-route packets are the small set that carry codes: 65 in my 24-hour window, 64 of them scoped. [[@cs-src-db:L3270-3299; cs-scope]] See Finding 8.

Use instead: "99.5% of all transmissions the analyzer observed (floods and direct packets together) carried no region scope."

§01Out of date

"~600 repeaters in West Coast Mesh, approaching the Q2 2026 reported count"

[[F]] The report said "we are approaching the 600 repeater mark" on 26 July. [[@wc-q2:L7, L90]] [[F]] The analyzer listed 806 repeaters, 183 companions and 48 room servers on 13 October. [[@cs-stats]] [[U]] The analyzer does not say how long an unheard node stays on its list, so 806 may include some that have gone quiet.

Use instead: "About 800 repeaters are known to the SoCal analyzer (806 on 13 Oct 2026). The Q2 report put the mesh near 600 in late July."

§01Out of date

"1,332/day reported channel-message total"

[[F]] Correct as the April to June average. [[@wc-q2:L37, L44]] [[C]] The week to 13 October ran at 2,846 a day on the analyzer (19,919 in 168 hours). [[@cs-rf]] [[U]] Method comparability unconfirmed.

Use instead: "1,332 channel messages a day on average in Q2. The analyzer shows about 2,850 a day for the week to 13 Oct."

§01Confirmed, caveat

"20 to 25%, the community's Q2 estimate of the overwhelm threshold"

[[F]] The number is right. The plan leaves out the first half of the sentence, "We are starting to hit that", and the unit, which is airtime utilization. [[@wc-q2:L39; wc-q1:L22]]

Use instead: "The Q2 report says the mesh is starting to hit the 20 to 25% airtime utilization at which it gets overwhelmed."

§01Confirmed, caveat

"SoCal uses 927.875 MHz, SF7, CR5, creating a hard RF boundary from the Bay Area, Central Valley, and Central Coast"

[[F]] SoCal: 927.875 MHz, 62.5 kHz, SF7, CR5. The other three are listed at 910.525 MHz. [[@wc-radio:L9-13]] [[F]] The plan omits the bandwidth, and WCMesh notes that coding rate may differ per device while "Every other parameter has to match." [[@wc-radio:L16]] [[C]] The two centre frequencies are 17.35 MHz apart. [[I]] "Hard RF boundary" follows from that; it is my reading and the plan's, and neither of us measured it.

§01Confirmed, caveat

"ScotMesh reports that roughly 24,000 daily flood packets from the wider UK mesh nearly overwhelmed its network before regions were introduced"

[[F]] The source says the mesh "was dying under the flood traffic from England (24,000 flood packets & 600+ messages daily)". [[@scot-regions:L15]] [[I]] The comparison to SoCal is weak: that was a neighbor's traffic arriving on a shared frequency. See Finding 7.

§01Unsupported

"The network is healthy." "...while load is still modest."

[[F]] The plan's own source says the mesh is starting to hit its overwhelm range, and live traffic is about double the quoted figure. [[@wc-q2:L39; cs-rf]] See Finding 2.

§02Right, wrong source

"Matching is per name, there is no inheritance, so each repeater must carry the entire intended ancestry." Cited to the CLI docs and the FAQ.

[[F]] Correct. The match loop tests each allowed name's code and never reads the parent field. [[@fw-regionmap:L190-204]] [[F]] The FAQ does not discuss scopes at all. [[@fw-docs-faq]] [[F]] The PNW guide says the same in plain words: the parent column "doesn't change forwarding". [[@pnw-rollout:L105]]

Cite instead: RegionMap.cpp at the release tag, lines 190 to 204.

§02Confirmed

"DMs with a known path are routed directly; only floods are scope-checked."

[[F]] The forwarding gate applies its region test only to flood-routed packets, and a direct packet's region is set to nothing on receive. [[@fw-rep:L434-443, L556-568]]

§02Confirmed

"2 × 16-bit codes"

[[F]] Two 16-bit codes are written after the header on transport routes. [[@fw-packet:L52-58]] [[F]] Current firmware always sends the second as zero; the source marks it for future use. [[@fw-rep:L997-1005]]

§02Confirmed, caveat

"Scope spelling does not cost extra airtime."

[[F]] True of the spelling: the name is never sent, only its code. [[@fw-tks:L4-18]] [[C]] Scoping itself costs 4 bytes per flood, about 10 ms or 4% on an average 56-byte packet at this preset. [[@fw-packet:L52-58; own-toa]]

§02Right, wrong source

"On Public, the message limit changes from 137 to 127 characters." Cited to the MeshCore FAQ.

[[F]] The figure is one Canadian test of the app, with the warning that it "can vary with your node name and app version". [[@ca-onqc:L729-732]] The FAQ has no such statement. [[@fw-docs-faq]]

Use instead: "The app allows slightly shorter messages on a scoped channel. Canada's testers saw Public drop from 137 to 127 characters; the exact limit depends on node name and app version."

§02Right, wrong source

"Nodes support 32 region entries, chains up to 8 levels, and codes up to 30 characters." Cited to the CLI docs.

[[F]] 32 and 30 come from the source, not the docs. [[@fw-regionmap-h:L7-8, L18]] [[F]] The firmware author publishes 29 bytes as the limit. [[@blog-region]] [[F]] "max 8 levels" is a property of region load indentation. [[@fw-docs-cli:L761]]

Use instead: "A repeater holds 32 region names besides the wildcard. Keep names to 29 bytes or fewer, lower case, letters, digits and hyphen."

§02Confirmed

"Regions exist from 1.10"

[[F]] The firmware author dates region config and filtering to the "repeater v1.10.0 release". [[@blog-region]] [[F]] That tag is dated 13 November 2025. [[@gh-tags]]

§02Confirmed, caveat

"use 1.15+ minimum and target 1.16+ for flood.max.unscoped"

[[F]] flood.max.unscoped arrived in 1.16.0, and WCMesh asks for 1.15.0 or newer. [[@blog-116; wc-q2:L95]] [[I]] The plan then gives no command that runs on 1.15. See Finding 11.

§02Confirmed

"Out of the box, a repeater carries only * (unscoped). region denyf * drops all unscoped floods and is therefore breaking. The gentler 1.16+ control is set flood.max.unscoped."

[[F]] The wildcard starts with flooding allowed and no other region exists by default. [[@fw-regionmap:L46-49]] [[F]] With the wildcard denied, an unscoped flood gets no region and is not forwarded. [[@fw-rep:L440-443, L559-564]] [[F]] The unscoped hop limit is a separate test. [[@fw-routing:L19]]

§02Confirmed, caveat

"Companion apps expose per-channel scope, Default Region Scope, and Tools → Discover Regions."

[[F]] Per-channel "Set Region Scope" since app 1.38.0; default scope in app 1.43.0 under Experimental Settings; "Tools → Discover Regions" per the PNW guide. [[@blog-region; blog-default; pnw-rollout:L156-161]] [[U]] Confirmed from those documents only. I did not inspect the app.

§03Confirmed, caveat

Role A: "Its default is the metro, so its own adverts remain local."

[[F]] Correct mechanics. [[@fw-rep:L1031-1038]] [[I]] "Local" means "forwarded only by repeaters that already hold the metro tag", which for a first mover is none. See Finding 4.

§03Confirmed

"RF reach does not create a scope boundary: a mountaintop can hear far while forwarding only matching tags."

[[F]] Forwarding depends on the region table alone. [[@fw-rep:L434-443]]

§03Right, wrong source

"A pathless DM floods under the default scope, so the default must be wide enough to find a recipient." Cited to the FAQ.

[[F]] Correct, from the firmware author's post and the companion source. [[@blog-default; fw-comp:L500-510]] The FAQ does not say it. [[@fw-docs-faq]]

§03Confirmed

"If a channel is scoped before every repeater on its path carries that tag, reach shrinks."

[[F]] Stated in the PNW troubleshooting section and visible in the forwarding gate. [[@pnw-rollout:L235; fw-rep:L440-443]]

§03Confirmed

"Never rename a published code. A rename silently splits a mesh."

[[F]] "The region names must be exact, any misspellings will be treated as a completely different region!" [[@blog-region]]

§03, RT-6Already answerable

"Use lowercase provisionally. Case behavior must pass the Phase-1 bench test before rollout."

[[F]] The published rule is lower case only, and repeater firmware treats case as significant. [[@blog-region; fw-regionmap:L52-55, L207-216]] [[U]] Only the app's handling of typed capitals is open. See Finding 11.

Use instead: "Region names are lower case. Repeaters treat OXR and oxr as different regions."
