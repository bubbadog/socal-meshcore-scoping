## Third-party data

`src/geo.json` holds county outlines and local-area reference points extracted from [matkam/meshcore-settings](https://github.com/matkam/meshcore-settings) (`data/outlines.json` and `data/regions.json` at commit 331ffd284381cd3b2db5b763a9e15f3defe8948f), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 matkam. That project states that its boundaries come from US Census TIGER/Line data via the click_that_hood project (MIT), and that Census boundary data is a work of the US government in the public domain.

`src/packets.json` holds the raw bytes of three flood adverts broadcast on a public radio channel by one repeater and served by the public analyzer at corescope.wcmesh.com.
